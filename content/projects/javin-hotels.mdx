JAVIN Hotels is a considered digital experience for a Port Harcourt hotel, designed to turn browsing into direct guest enquiries. The site presents rooms, facilities, gatherings, location details, and a seamless path to check availability.

### Key features

* **Room discovery:** Presents guest rooms and suites with a clear path to explore every stay.
* **Availability search:** Lets guests specify check-in, check-out, and guest count before beginning an enquiry.
* **Direct reservations:** Connects guests to the hotel through WhatsApp for fast, personal booking support.
* **Complete hotel information:** Brings together facilities, events, gallery, contact details, and directions in one cohesive experience.

### Experience focus

The design balances visual storytelling with useful booking information, giving guests a warm, polished first impression while keeping direct contact and conversion within easy reach.
