FinVault is a premium fintech demonstration built around confidence, simplicity, and a focused saving experience. Its guided onboarding introduces secure savings, instant transfers, and bank-grade protection before taking users into the product.

### Key features

* **Goal-based savings:** A clear, future-focused way to help users build better saving habits.
* **Transfers:** Designed to make moving money feel quick and straightforward.
* **Demo-safe experience:** Clearly communicates that no real money is involved and lets users reset demo data.
* **Installable web app:** PWA support gives the product a fast, native-app-like experience on supported devices.

### Experience focus

The interface pairs a calm, premium visual system with mobile-first onboarding and security-forward messaging, making a complex financial product feel approachable.
